Governance
==

The XML Standards Subcommittee is a subcommittee of the USAHA [Animal Health Surveillance and Information Systems Committee](https://usaha.org/animal-health-surveillance-and-information). The subcommittee operates under the established bylaws, policies, and procedures of the United States Animal Health Association (USAHA).
